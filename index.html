Privacy notice

How verbAfrica handles the information you submit through this site. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Who we are. verbAfrica is a curated South African creative booking directory. For any privacy request, contact alexander@verbafrica.co.za.

What we collect. Only what you type into the enquiry, brief or get-involved forms — your name, your contact detail (email and/or phone) and your message. We don't use tracking cookies or analytics. A shortlist you build stays only in your own browser.

Why. To respond to your enquiry, match you with creatives and run the booking service. We rely on the consent you give by ticking the box.

Where it goes. Submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet and emailed to us so we can act on them. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers (Google, GitHub) may process and store data on servers outside South Africa, so submitting involves a cross-border transfer you consent to.

How long. We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to provide the service and for reasonable business records, then delete them.

Your rights. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing alexander@verbafrica.co.za.
